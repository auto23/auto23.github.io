Privacy policy

sysmon is a personal, internal tool. It uploads system log files and inspection images from the owner's own machine to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Scope used: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (only files created by the app).
  • The app does not read, collect, sell or share any other Google user data.
  • No data is sent to any third party. Credentials are stored only on the owner's machine.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
